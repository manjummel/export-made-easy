1. FOREIGN TRADE POLICY (FTP) 2023-2028
=============================================================
The Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) 2023-28 was notified by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry on April 1, 2023. The policy is aimed at raising India's goods and services exports to USD 2 trillion by 2030.

Key Schemes:
- RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products): Provides rebate of central, state, and local duties/taxes/levies on exports at 0.5% to 4.3% of FOB value depending on product category.
- RoSCTL (Rebate of State and Central Taxes and Levies): Applicable for textile and apparel exporters.
- EPCG (Export Promotion Capital Goods Scheme): Allows import of capital goods at 0% customs duty for export production with an export obligation of 6x the duty saved.
- Advance Authorisation: Allows duty-free import of inputs for export production.
- Star Export House: Recognition scheme rewarding high-volume exporters (1-star to 5-star status).

Export Obligation: Exporters under Advance Authorisation or EPCG must fulfil export obligations within stipulated timeframes, typically 18-24 months.

2. HS CODE CLASSIFICATION (ITC-HS 2026)
=============================================================
India follows the ITC-HS (Indian Trade Classification based on Harmonized System) for tariff classification. The schedule is maintained by DGFT and CBIC.

Common Export HS Codes:
- Chapter 62: Articles of apparel and clothing, not knitted or crocheted
  - HS 6205.20.00: Men's woven cotton shirts
  - HS 6204.62.00: Women's trousers and shorts of cotton
- Chapter 09: Coffee, tea, spices
  - HS 0910.30.00: Turmeric (Curcuma)
  - HS 0901.11.00: Coffee, not roasted, not decaffeinated
- Chapter 84: Nuclear reactors, boilers, machinery
  - HS 8471.30: Portable computers (laptops)
- Chapter 27: Mineral fuels, oils
  - HS 2709.00: Crude petroleum oils
- Chapter 71: Natural or cultured pearls, precious metals
  - HS 7113.19.10: Jewellery of gold

Export Restrictions:
- Items on the Prohibited List cannot be exported under any circumstances
- Items on the Restricted List require a license from DGFT
- Freely exportable items (most items) can be exported without a license

3. INDIA-UAE COMPREHENSIVE ECONOMIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT (CEPA)
=============================================================
The India-UAE CEPA came into effect on May 1, 2022. It is India's most significant FTA in recent decades.

Key Benefits:
- Tariff Elimination: 97% of India's exports to UAE get 0% duty immediately or over 5-10 years
- Textile and Apparel: Immediate duty elimination on HS Chapter 61 and 62 products (cotton shirts, garments)
- Rules of Origin (RoO): Products must satisfy 35% Qualifying Value Content (QVC) or undergo substantial transformation to be eligible
- Certificate of Origin: Form AI Certificate of Origin issued by designated agencies (FIEO, Export Inspection Councils, Chambers of Commerce)
- Service Sectors: 111 sub-sectors covered including IT, banking, healthcare

Textile Products:
- Men's cotton shirts (HS 6205.20): 0% duty at UAE ports with valid Form AI COO
- Women's garments (HS 6204): 0% duty, QVC > 35% from India
- Home textiles (HS 6302): 0% duty

4. INDIA-EU GENERALIZED SCHEME OF PREFERENCES (GSP)
=============================================================
India exports to EU under the EU GSP scheme providing preferential tariff rates.

Note: As of December 2023, India graduated from EU GSP as a higher-middle income economy. Exporters should verify current tariff rates under MFN (Most Favoured Nation) or check bilateral FTA progress between India and EU.

EU Import Requirements:
- REACH Regulation: Chemicals must comply with EU REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals)
- CE Marking: Required for electrical goods, machinery, medical devices
- EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation): Effective 2025, affects timber, soy, palm oil, cocoa, coffee, rubber, cattle
- Food Safety: EU Food Safety Regulations for agricultural products

5. DGFT — DIRECTORATE GENERAL OF FOREIGN TRADE
=============================================================
Website: https://www.dgft.gov.in

Key Functions:
- Issues IEC (Importer Exporter Code) — mandatory for all import/export activities
- Notifies Foreign Trade Policy and procedures
- Issues Advance Authorisation, EPCG, and other export promotion licenses
- Manages SION (Standard Input Output Norms) for duty-free imports
- Operates ICEDASH for export data monitoring

IEC (Importer Exporter Code):
- 10-digit code mandatory for all exporters and importers
- Applied online at DGFT portal
- Linked to PAN of proprietor/company
- No renewal required, valid for lifetime

SION (Standard Input Output Norms):
- Defines input-output ratios for products under Advance Authorisation
- Updated regularly by DGFT based on industry inputs

6. CBIC AND CUSTOMS PROCEDURES
=============================================================
Website: https://www.cbic.gov.in

Customs Duty:
- Basic Customs Duty (BCD): Standard import tariff
- Integrated GST (IGST): Applied on imports, creditable against GST liability
- Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS): 10% on BCD
- Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD): Applied on specific products to prevent dumping

Export Customs Procedure:
1. Registration: Obtain IEC from DGFT
2. Shipping Bill: File electronically through ICEGATE (Indian Customs EDI Gateway)
3. Examination: Customs may examine goods physically or through scanning
4. Let Export Order: Customs issues LEO (Let Export Order) after verification
5. Export General Manifest (EGM): Filed by shipping line/airline after departure

Green Channel: Low-risk exporters get automated clearance without physical examination.

Duty Drawback:
- Refund of customs duty paid on imported inputs used in export production
- All Industry Rate (AIR) or Brand Rate drawback available
- Applied through ICEGATE customs portal

7. ICEGATE — INDIAN CUSTOMS ELECTRONIC GATEWAY
=============================================================
Website: https://www.icegate.gov.in

Services:
- Online filing of Shipping Bills for export
- Filing of Bills of Entry for import
- Payment of customs duty online
- e-Sanchit: Paperless document submission for customs clearance
- Risk Management System (RMS): Automated risk profiling for cargo

Shipping Bill:
- Electronic document filed by exporter/CHA before export
- Contains: exporter details, buyer details, product description, HS code, quantity, value
- Types: Free Shipping Bill, Drawback Shipping Bill, DEEC Shipping Bill

8. RBI — FOREIGN EXCHANGE MANAGEMENT (FEMA)
=============================================================
Website: https://www.rbi.org.in

Export Realisation:
- Full export proceeds must be realised within 9 months from date of export (extended from 180 days)
- Exports to countries not listed in SWIFT can use alternative payment methods
- EDPMS (Export Data Processing and Monitoring System): RBI system tracking export bill realisations

Forward Contracts:
- Exporters can book forward contracts to hedge against forex risk
- Available for up to 12 months
- Rollover permitted with proper documentation

Export Finance:
- PCFC (Pre-Shipment Credit in Foreign Currency): Low interest rate export finance from banks
- PSCF (Post-Shipment Credit in Foreign Currency): Finance against export documents
- ECGC (Export Credit Guarantee Corporation): Insurance against buyer default risk

9. APEDA — AGRICULTURAL AND PROCESSED FOOD PRODUCTS EXPORT DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY
=============================================================
Website: https://www.apeda.gov.in

Mandated Products:
- Fruits and vegetables, floriculture
- Meat and meat products
- Poultry and dairy products
- Cereals and processed foods
- Spices (shared mandate with Spices Board)

Registration:
- All exporters of APEDA mandated products must register with APEDA
- APEDA registration valid for 5 years

Quality Standards:
- EIC (Export Inspection Council) certification for food safety
- HACCP, ISO 22000 certification requirements for EU/US exports
- Residue Monitoring Plan (RMP) compliance required for export to EU

10. RISK MANAGEMENT FOR EXPORTERS
=============================================================

Foreign Exchange Risk:
- USD/INR and EUR/INR are primary currency pairs for Indian exports
- Forward contract hedge recommended for receivables > $50,000
- Options contracts available for higher flexibility
- Natural hedge: Match USD receivables with USD payables

Credit Risk:
- ECGC Policy: Insurance against buyer default
- Letter of Credit (LC): Preferred payment instrument for high-value exports
- Document Against Payment (DP): Suitable for trusted buyers
- Open Account: High risk, only for long-standing relationships

Country Risk:
- ECGC Country Risk Classification: A1 to C3 ratings
- Avoid Open Account terms with C2/C3 rated countries
- Consult ECGC for country-specific limits

Compliance Risk:
- Denied Party Screening: Check against US BIS, OFAC, EU Sanctions lists
- Import prohibitions: Verify if product is banned/restricted in destination country
- Labelling requirements: Country-specific label language requirements

11. LOGISTICS AND SHIPPING
=============================================================

Major Indian Export Ports:
- JNPT (Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust), Navi Mumbai: Largest container port
- Mundra Port, Gujarat: Private container port
- Chennai Port: South India hub
- Vizag Port (VCTPL): Bulk and container
- Kolkata/Haldia: Eastern India

Air Cargo:
- IGI Airport, Delhi: Major air cargo hub
- CSIA, Mumbai: Second largest air cargo hub
- Deferred rates available for non-perishable cargo

Incoterms 2020:
- FOB (Free on Board): Seller delivers at port of export
- CIF (Cost Insurance Freight): Seller responsible until destination port
- DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): Seller responsible for all costs including import duty
- EXW (Ex Works): Buyer takes all responsibility from seller's premises

Freight Forwarders:
- CHA (Custom House Agent): Licensed by Customs to file shipping documents
- NVOCC (Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier): Consolidates shipments

12. PAYMENT TERMS AND DOCUMENTATION
=============================================================

Export Documents:
1. Commercial Invoice: Details of goods, value, terms
2. Packing List: Detailed packing information
3. Bill of Lading (BL): Receipt from shipping company
4. Certificate of Origin: Proves goods originate from India
5. Shipping Bill: Customs clearance document
6. Letter of Credit: Bank guarantee of payment
7. GATT Declaration: Buyer's confirmation of end use

Additional Documents (as required):
- Phytosanitary Certificate: For agricultural products (issued by Plant Quarantine)
- Health Certificate: For food products (issued by EIC/APEDA)
- Fumigation Certificate: Pest treatment certificate
- Quality Inspection Certificate: Third-party quality verification
- Weight Certificate: For bulk commodities
